### 校对时容易混淆的题

- **第 1 套听力 13、16、23、24：**依次为 **D、B、C、B**。所问分别是从不同角度理解新岗位、消费者未实际比较品牌和价格、宠物带来的目标感、以及养宠的缺点。回忆版的文字与本卷选项含义相符，但字母对应不同。
- **第 2 套听力 9、14、20：**依次为 **D、B、C**，分别对应出差、通过社交媒体与朋友联系、癌症和肥胖风险更高。
- **第 1 套阅读 26–28：**分别为 **N（squeezed）、A（available）、I（increased）**。第 27 空修饰商品，第 28 空的主语是生活水平，需填谓语。
- **第 2 套阅读 29：**为 **G（heightened）**，表示更强的味觉敏感度。
- **第 3 套阅读 42：**为 **K**。K 段强调答得清楚简洁；I 段着重提醒减少口头语。
- **第 3 套阅读 50：**按本卷题面，**A** 更贴合结论。文中讨论儿童能否理解道德厌恶，并未测定八岁以下儿童产生这种情绪的频率。
- **阅读题措辞提示：**第 1 套第 52 题选 **B** 为给定选项中最接近者，原文支持遗传对行为有影响，也强调环境的作用。第 2 套第 50 题选 **A** 为最接近者，抑郁易感性在末段出现，但该段未明确将此结论单独归给题干提及的两位心理学家。

## 第 1 套：学习示例

### 写作：校园机器人未来应用

Robots could make university life easier by taking over routine tasks. For example, a robot in the library could guide visitors to the right shelves or help staff return books to their places. On a large campus, delivery robots could carry laboratory supplies between buildings, leaving staff more time for work that requires their judgment.

The university should start with a small trial in places where the need is clear. Students and staff could report problems with routes, waiting times, or accessibility. The university should also explain what information the robots collect and keep a human service desk available for anyone who needs personal help.

Campus robots would be most useful if they solved specific problems. A trial would let the university see which services students actually use before investing in more machines.

### 翻译：尊师与教师节

An old Chinese saying compares a teacher, even for a single day, to a father for life. In Chinese culture, teachers pass on knowledge and teach students how to conduct themselves. In ancient times, students held a formal ceremony when they first met their teacher, reflecting the high regard in which teachers were held. To preserve the tradition of honoring teachers, China has observed Teachers' Day on September 10 each year since 1985. On that day, students show their respect in different ways. Respect for teachers is a traditional Chinese virtue that continues to be promoted and practiced today.

## 第 2 套：学习示例

### 写作：如何让校园生活更便利

I propose that the university make its existing campus services easier to use. To begin with, it could publish the opening hours of libraries, clinics, and dining halls on a single page. Students would then know where to go without checking several websites or making unnecessary trips.

The university could also extend the opening hours of one study room during exam weeks. Before making the change permanent, it should ask students when they actually need a quiet place to study and record how many people use the room at different times.

Finally, a simple reporting form would help students notify staff about broken lights or other problems. If each report showed who was handling it and when a repair was expected, students would spend less time following up. These changes would make daily life more convenient without requiring new buildings.

### 翻译：餐桌礼仪

Dining etiquette is an important part of traditional Chinese culture and reflects the warmth and courtesy shown to guests. A host prepares a generous selection of carefully made dishes to suit the guests' tastes. Seating is arranged with particular care: the host sits facing the door, and the guest of honor sits beside the host. During the meal, the host occasionally refills the guests' wine glasses and serves them more food. These customs have survived for thousands of years and have played an important role in fostering harmonious relationships.

## 第 3 套：学习示例

### 写作：要求大学生参与科研项目的必要性与可行性

I support giving every university student experience in at least one research project, provided that the requirement can be met in different ways. Research teaches students to turn a broad question into something they can investigate, evaluate evidence, and explain why their conclusions might be limited. These skills are useful even for students who do not plan to become researchers.

The requirement would be practical if projects were small enough to fit into ordinary courses. A group could analyze a public dataset, survey campus transport, or compare sources on a local historical question. Teachers could guide several groups using common milestones, such as a proposal and a short presentation.

Universities should offer alternatives for students whose courses or work schedules make a laboratory placement difficult. With reasonable workloads and clear guidance, the requirement could give students a worthwhile introduction to research.

### 翻译：礼尚往来

Exchanging gifts among relatives and friends is an important social custom in traditional Chinese culture. A gift conveys affection as well as offering something of material value, and it helps sustain relationships. A thoughtfully chosen gift that suits the recipient can strengthen the bond between the giver and the recipient. Following the tradition of returning courtesies, people in China thank the giver when receiving a gift and offer one in return at an appropriate time. This exchange helps maintain good relations.
