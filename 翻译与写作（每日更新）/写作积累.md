### 001 城市生活与自然

**材料类型：**英语六级写作　　**整理日期：**2026-10-09　　**原题来源：**2026年6月大学英语六级考试真题（第1套）

**材料说明：**个人英文作文此前已提供；2026-10-18根据用户上传的试卷图片补录2026年6月大学英语六级考试真题（第1套）的完整写作要求。下面保留个人原文，再给出便于学习的中文理解和轻度润色版，不推断考试得分。

#### 作文题目（图片转录）

> **Directions:** For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence “With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature.” You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.
>
> **You should copy the sentence given in quotes at the beginning of your essay.**

**要求提要：**30分钟；150—200词；开头必须逐字抄写引号中的指定句。下方个人原文使用了指定开头句。

#### 用户提供的英文原文

With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature. As cities grow, many people spend more time indoors and have fewer chances to enjoy the natural world. This makes it necessary to bring nature back into our daily lives.

First, spending time in nature can help people reduce stress. For example, taking a walk in a park after work allows them to relax and forget their worries for a while. Second, a love for nature can encourage residents to protect the environment. People who value green spaces are more likely to save water, reduce waste and take part in activities such as planting trees. These small actions can make cities better places to live.

Therefore, both individuals and local governments should take action. Residents can spend more time outdoors and learn about plants and animals. Meanwhile, governments can build more parks and organize environmental activities. By working together, we can enjoy healthier lives and create greener cities.

#### 中文理解（根据英文整理，不是另行提供的中文原题）

随着城市化的发展，城市居民培养更强的自然热爱之情十分重要。随着城市扩张，许多人在室内待的时间更长，享受自然的机会更少。因此，有必要把自然重新带回日常生活。

首先，亲近自然可以帮助人们减轻压力。例如，下班后在公园散步能让人放松，暂时忘记烦恼。其次，热爱自然可以鼓励居民保护环境。珍视绿地的人更可能节约用水、减少浪费，并参加植树等活动。这些小小的行动可以让城市变得更加宜居。

因此，个人和地方政府都应采取行动。居民可以多在户外活动，了解植物和动物。与此同时，政府可以建设更多公园并组织环保活动。通过共同努力，我们能够享有更健康的生活，并建设更绿色的城市。

#### 学习用润色版

As urbanization continues, it is important for city residents to develop a greater appreciation for nature. As cities grow, many people spend more time indoors and have fewer chances to enjoy the natural world. This makes it necessary to bring nature back into our daily lives.

First, spending time in nature can help people reduce stress. For example, taking a walk in a park after work allows them to relax and forget their worries for a while. Second, a love for nature can encourage residents to protect the environment. People who value green spaces are more likely to save water, reduce waste and take part in activities such as planting trees. These small actions can make cities better places to live.

Therefore, both individuals and local governments should take action. Residents can spend more time outdoors and learn about plants and animals. Meanwhile, governments can build more parks and organize environmental activities. By working together, we can enjoy healthier lives and create greener cities.

**修改说明：**原文整体语法和结构清楚。本版主要将开头改为更自然的 `As urbanization continues`，并使用 `develop a greater appreciation for nature` 表达“更加欣赏、重视自然”。但真题明确要求作文以指定句开头，因此该润色版只用于比较表达，不能直接作为本题考场版本；正式作答必须保留原题指定开头。原来的 `develop a stronger love for nature` 可以理解；初学时先记 `develop a love for...` 即可。`vitally important` 语法正确，只是语气更强。不把润色选择当作唯一正确答案。

#### 写作结构与可迁移思路

| 段落 | 本篇功能 | 可以模仿的做法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 第一段 | 城市发展背景 → 接触自然的机会减少 → 提出观点 | 先写一个具体变化，再说明为什么需要行动，不只写空泛的“非常重要” |
| 第二段 | 减轻压力 + 保护环境，两点理由 | 每个理由配一个例子或具体行为，如散步、节水、植树 |

本篇的 `First / Second / For example / Therefore / Meanwhile` 各有作用。模仿时保留逻辑，不必把所有连接词都机械塞入每一篇作文。

#### 本篇优先背诵清单（12 项）

1. it is important for somebody to do something（对某人来说，做某事很重要）
2. develop a love for...（培养对……的热爱）
3. spend time in nature（在自然中度过时间／亲近自然）
4. have fewer chances to do something（有更少的机会做某事）
5. reduce stress（减轻压力）
6. allow somebody to do something（允许／使某人有条件做某事）
7. encourage somebody to do something（鼓励／促使某人做某事）
8. protect the environment（保护环境）
9. be more likely to do something（更有可能做某事）
10. take part in...（参加……）
11. take action to do something（采取行动做某事）
12. by working together（通过共同努力）

先背这12项；`urbanization`、`appreciation` 等拓展词可稍后复习。旧材料中的 `help somebody do / to do something` 与 `make it + adjective + to do something` 在本篇继续复现，已在总表合并来源。

<a id="writing-notes-001"></a>
#### 关键语法与易错点

1. **`it is important for somebody to do something`。** `it` 是形式主语，后面的不定式说明具体事项：`It is important for residents to protect nature.` 练习本句型时不要漏掉 `for` 或 `to`，不能写 `It is important for residents protect nature.` 原文 `vitally important` 中的 `vitally` 是副词，修饰形容词 `important`；不能改成 `vital important`。
2. **`develop` 的新用法。** 在翻译001中可表示发展、制定；本篇 `develop a love for nature` 指逐渐形成或培养情感，不是“开发自然”。`development` 是名词，不能代替动词：`We can develop a love for reading.`
3. **`spend` 后面的结构。** `spend time indoors / outdoors` 用地点副词；`spend time in nature` 用介词短语；花时间做事用 `spend time doing something`，不用 `spend time to do something`。`outdoors` 是副词，`outdoor` 是形容词：`spend time outdoors` / `outdoor activities`。
4. **`fewer` 与 `less`。** `chances` 是复数可数名词，因此用 `fewer chances`；`time` 在此不可数，因此用 `less time`。不写 `less chances`。
5. **`make it necessary to do something`。** 与翻译001的 `make it easier for somebody to do something` 共用 `make + it + 形容词 + (for + 人) + to do` 结构；`for + 人` 可按需要省略。`This makes it necessary to bring nature back into our daily lives.` 中 `it` 是形式宾语；`This` 作主语，谓语用 `makes`。
6. **`help / allow / encourage / enable` 的区别。** `help somebody do / to do` 两种都可用；`allow / encourage / enable somebody to do` 在这些句型中都保留 `to`。它们分别侧重帮助、许可或提供条件、鼓励、使能够，不能因为中文接近就任意互换。
7. **动名词短语作主语。** `Spending time in nature helps people relax.` 和 `Taking a walk allows them to relax.` 中，整个动作短语通常看作单数，分别用 `helps`、`allows`。原文 `spending time ... can help` 中，情态动词 `can` 后用动词原形。
8. **`be more likely to do` 表示可能性。** `People who value green spaces are more likely to protect them.` 中 `people` 是复数，关系从句用 `value`，主句用 `are`；该表达不等于“必然会”。
9. **并列与举例。** 原文 `save water, reduce waste and take part in...` 三个动词形式并列；`such as planting trees` 中用动名词。对比：`outdoor activities such as walking` / `For example, residents can walk in a park.`
10. **`by` 后接动名词。** `By working together, we can create greener cities.` 不用 `by work` 或 `by to work`；共同努力的人是后面的 `we`。换主语时也要保证逻辑一致。
11. **让某地变得宜居。** `make cities better places to live` 是 `make + 宾语 + 名词补足语`：复数 `cities` 对应 `places`；单数可写 `make our city a better place to live`。
12. **词形与连接词。** `environment` 是名词，`environmental` 是形容词；用 `protect the environment`、`environmental activities`。`Therefore` 表示因果，`Meanwhile` 表示同时或并行，不合并成同一种逻辑连接词。

### 003 传统文化与文化自信

**材料类型：**写作　　**题目信息：**2026年6月英语六级写作第3套（按用户提供文本整理）　　**整理日期：**2026-10-18

#### 题目要求与中文理解

> **Directions:** For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence: “Faced with growing globalization, students realize that a deep understanding of traditional culture helps cultivate cultural confidence.” You can make comments, cite examples or use your experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.

**中文理解：**面对日益发展的全球化，学生们意识到，深入理解传统文化有助于培养文化自信。

**作答要求：**30分钟；150—200词；必须在作文开头原样使用指定英文句子，可发表评论、举例或结合个人经历。指定句应计入全文词数。

#### 三段式写作结构（学习指导，不是个人作文）

1. **引出主题：**先原样写指定开头句，再表明观点。例如：In my opinion, traditional culture plays an important role in helping young people develop a strong sense of cultural identity.
2. **分析原因：**其一，学习传统文化有助于建立文化认同：First of all, learning about traditional culture enables students to develop a stronger sense of cultural identity. 其二，了解传统文化有助于跨文化交流：Furthermore, a deep understanding of traditional culture allows students to introduce Chinese culture to people from different countries. 可用传统节日举例：For example, students can introduce traditional Chinese festivals, such as the Spring Festival and the Mid-Autumn Festival, to their foreign friends.
3. **总结建议：**回扣观点并提出可行行动。例如：In conclusion, traditional culture is essential to developing cultural confidence. Therefore, students should actively learn about Chinese traditions and share them with the world.

这些句子是可选择的示范素材，写作时应根据论证需要取舍，不能把它们拼接成一篇已完成作文。

#### 核心词汇与搭配（20项）

下表是本题学习视图；与旧词条重合的表达只在此补充本题用法，不再作为独立的总表新词条。`enable somebody to do something` 已见[翻译积累的共用表达](./翻译积累.md#translation-patterns)。

| 英文表达 | 中文释义 | 用法 | 可复用例句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| globalization | 全球化 | 不可数名词；`growing globalization` 表示全球化发展。 | Globalization creates new opportunities for cultural exchange. |
| traditional culture | 传统文化 | `traditional` 为形容词，修饰 `culture`。 | Schools can help students learn about traditional culture. |
| cultural confidence | 文化自信 | `cultural` 为形容词；常与 `develop`、`cultivate` 连用。 | Learning local history can strengthen cultural confidence. |
| cultural identity | 文化认同 | 常见搭配 `a sense of cultural identity`。 | Family stories can give children a sense of cultural identity. |
| cultivate | 培养 | 可接 `confidence`、`habits` 等名词。 | Reading can cultivate curiosity in young people. |
| strengthen | 增强 | 可接 `confidence`、`cooperation` 等名词。 | Team projects can strengthen students' communication skills. |
| enable somebody to do something | 使某人能够做某事 | `enable + 宾语 + to + 动词原形`；复用已有跨册表达。 | Digital libraries enable students to access books from home. |
| play an important role in | 在……中发挥重要作用 | `in` 后接名词或动名词。 | Exercise plays an important role in maintaining good health. |
| a deep understanding of | 对……的深入理解 | `understanding` 为名词，后接 `of + 对象`。 | A deep understanding of local needs helps improve public services. |
| introduce A to B | 向B介绍A | `A` 是介绍的事物，`B` 是接受介绍的人。 | Volunteers introduce local history to visitors. |
| cultural exchange | 文化交流 | 可说 `promote cultural exchange`。 | Travel can promote cultural exchange. |
| traditional Chinese festivals | 中国传统节日 | 具体节日举例时可用 `such as`。 | Schools can organize events about traditional Chinese festivals. |
| the Spring Festival | 春节 | 节日名称首字母大写，前用 `the`。 | Many families gather during the Spring Festival. |
| the Mid-Autumn Festival | 中秋节 | 节日名称首字母大写，前用 `the`。 | People often share mooncakes during the Mid-Autumn Festival. |
| be essential to doing something | 对做某事至关重要 | 这里的 `to` 是介词，后接动名词。 | Clean water is essential to protecting public health. |
| develop a sense of identity | 建立认同感 | `develop + a sense of + 名词`。 | Community activities help children develop a sense of identity. |
| broaden one's horizons | 拓宽视野 | `one's` 按主语变化；`horizons` 通常用复数。 | Reading widely can broaden students' horizons. |
| preserve traditional culture | 保护传统文化 | `preserve` 强调保护并传承。 | Museums help preserve traditional culture. |
| promote cultural understanding | 促进文化理解 | `promote + 名词短语`。 | Student exchanges can promote cultural understanding. |
| share Chinese culture with the world | 向世界传播中国文化 | `share A with B`，介词用 `with`。 | Young artists can share Chinese culture with the world. |

#### 可复用写作句型（8项）

下表是本题的句型应用视图。`Therefore` 和 `enable...to do...` 在旧资料中已有基础用法，此处仅补充新场景；句型可迁移，但不能机械拼接。

| 英文模板 | 中文意思 | 使用场景 | 原创例句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| In my opinion, ... plays an important role in ... | 我认为……在……中发挥重要作用 | 提出观点 | In my opinion, regular exercise plays an important role in maintaining good health. |
| One important reason is that ... | 一个重要原因是…… | 分析原因 | One important reason is that online courses give rural students more learning opportunities. |
| ... enables somebody to do something. | ……使某人能够做某事 | 说明作用 | Digital tools enable farmers to reach more customers. |
| Furthermore, ... | 此外，…… | 补充论点 | Furthermore, public transport can reduce traffic congestion. |
| For example, ... | 例如，…… | 举例支撑论点 | For example, schools can organize recycling activities. |
| In conclusion, ... | 总之，…… | 收束观点 | In conclusion, reading helps students think more independently. |
| Therefore, students should ... | 因此，学生应当…… | 根据前文提出建议 | Therefore, students should learn to check sources before sharing information. |
| ... is essential to doing something. | ……对做某事至关重要 | 强调必要条件 | Adequate sleep is essential to maintaining good health. |

#### 写作注意事项

1. 指定开头句必须原样使用；全文控制在150—200词，写作时间30分钟。
2. 优先保证切题、三段结构清楚和逻辑完整，避免为了“高级表达”牺牲语法准确性。
3. 检查主谓一致、时态、冠词和介词；举例应支持论点，避免重复表述相同观点。
4. `be essential to doing something` 中的 `to` 是介词，后接动名词：Traditional culture is essential to developing cultural confidence.

### 004｜2024年6月第1套｜数字素养与数字技能

**材料类型：**六级写作议论文　　**英文主题：**Digital Literacy and Skills　　**学习日期：**2026-10-23　　**题目来源：**按本次提供的题目文本整理。

#### 真题信息与题目要求

There is a growing awareness of the importance of digital literacy and skills in today's world. In my opinion, these abilities are essential for students to study effectively and prepare for their future careers.

First of all, digital skills help students obtain information and learn more efficiently. For example, they can use online libraries to find useful materials and attend courses offered by universities around the world. However, students should also learn to judge whether online information is reliable. Otherwise, they may be misled by false or inaccurate content. Furthermore, digital literacy plays an important role in the workplace. Students who master these skills will be better prepared for future challenges. In addition, protecting personal information is another important part of digital literacy. By understanding online risks, students can use the Internet more safely and responsibly.

In conclusion, digital literacy is not only a useful skill but also a necessary ability in modern society. Therefore, universities should provide more practical training, while students should actively improve their digital skills. Only in this way can young people make better use of technology and achieve personal development.

#### 可复用写作句型（8项）

| 英文模板 | 中文用途 | 语法注意事项 | 可迁移例句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| In my opinion, ... is/are essential for ... | 表达某事的重要性 | 主语单数用 is、复数用 are；for 后接名词或动名词。 | In my opinion, regular exercise is essential for good health. |
| ... plays an important role in ... | 分析某事的作用 | play 随主语变形；in 后接名词或动名词。 | Public transport plays an important role in reducing pollution. |
| For example, ... | 举例支持前文观点 | 例子须与论点相关；句首短语后用逗号。 | For example, schools can organize community cleanups. |
| As ... develops rapidly, ... | 引出技术或社会变化的背景 | as 引导从句；主句须有完整主语与谓语。 | As online education develops rapidly, more students can learn from home. |
| Students who ... will be better prepared for ... | 强调技能带来的未来优势 | who 从句修饰 students；主句用 will be；prepared 后接 for。 | Students who practise teamwork will be better prepared for future jobs. |
| ... is not only ... but also ... | 强调两个并列方面 | not only 与 but also 后的语法成分应平行。 | Reading is not only enjoyable but also useful for learning. |
| Therefore, ... should ... | 根据前文提出建议 | therefore 表示因果；should 后接动词原形。 | Therefore, cities should create more green spaces. |
| Only in this way can ... | 强调只有这样才能达到目标 | 句首 only + 状语引发主句部分倒装：can + 主语 + 动词原形。 | Only in this way can communities reduce waste. |

#### 语法注意事项

1. `these abilities are essential for ...` 的主语是复数，谓语用 `are`；讲单数事物时改用 `is`。`be essential for + 名词/动名词` 与写作003的 `be essential to doing something` 都可用，注意介词后的形式。
2. `Students who master these skills will ...` 中 `who` 引导定语从句；主句谓语为 `will be`。
3. `not only ... but also ...` 两侧保持语法平行；`Only in this way` 置于句首时，主句用 `can + 主语 + 动词原形`。
4. 题目要求的150—200词不含指定句；提交个人作文时仍需重新统计，不直接套用本篇参考范文的词数。

## 内容维护说明

1. 翻译材料与写作材料分别更新，两份文档各自从001开始独立编号。
2. 重复主题优先补充原章节；共用词汇和句型通过相对链接复用，避免重复维护。
3. 保留题目、个人作文、学习参考版本、例句、问题记录、练习和复习标记。
4. 只有题目而没有个人作文时明确标注“待提供”，不记录虚构的正文、评分或完成状态。
5. 新增内容后检查 UTF-8 编码、表格、材料编号、章节锚点和跨文档链接。
