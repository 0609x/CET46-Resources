### 002 城市生活与自然

**材料类型：**写作　　**整理日期：**2026-10-09

**材料说明：**个人英文作文此前已提供；2026-10-18根据用户上传的试卷图片补录2026年6月大学英语六级考试真题（第1套）的完整写作要求。下面保留个人原文，再给出便于学习的中文理解和轻度润色版，不推断考试得分。

#### 作文题目（图片转录）

> **Directions:** For this part, you are allowed 30 minutes to write an essay that begins with the sentence “With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature.” You can make comments, cite examples or use your personal experiences to develop your essay. You should write at least 150 words but no more than 200 words.
>
> **You should copy the sentence given in quotes at the beginning of your essay.**

**要求提要：**30分钟；150—200词；开头必须逐字抄写引号中的指定句。下方个人原文使用了指定开头句。

#### 用户提供的英文原文

With the trend of urbanization, it is vitally important for city residents to develop a stronger love for nature. As cities grow, many people spend more time indoors and have fewer chances to enjoy the natural world. This makes it necessary to bring nature back into our daily lives.

First, spending time in nature can help people reduce stress. For example, taking a walk in a park after work allows them to relax and forget their worries for a while. Second, a love for nature can encourage residents to protect the environment. People who value green spaces are more likely to save water, reduce waste and take part in activities such as planting trees. These small actions can make cities better places to live.

Therefore, both individuals and local governments should take action. Residents can spend more time outdoors and learn about plants and animals. Meanwhile, governments can build more parks and organize environmental activities. By working together, we can enjoy healthier lives and create greener cities.

#### 中文理解（根据英文整理，不是另行提供的中文原题）

随着城市化的发展，城市居民培养更强的自然热爱之情十分重要。随着城市扩张，许多人在室内待的时间更长，享受自然的机会更少。因此，有必要把自然重新带回日常生活。

首先，亲近自然可以帮助人们减轻压力。例如，下班后在公园散步能让人放松，暂时忘记烦恼。其次，热爱自然可以鼓励居民保护环境。珍视绿地的人更可能节约用水、减少浪费，并参加植树等活动。这些小小的行动可以让城市变得更加宜居。

因此，个人和地方政府都应采取行动。居民可以多在户外活动，了解植物和动物。与此同时，政府可以建设更多公园并组织环保活动。通过共同努力，我们能够享有更健康的生活，并建设更绿色的城市。

#### 学习用润色版

As urbanization continues, it is important for city residents to develop a greater appreciation for nature. As cities grow, many people spend more time indoors and have fewer chances to enjoy the natural world. This makes it necessary to bring nature back into our daily lives.

First, spending time in nature can help people reduce stress. For example, taking a walk in a park after work allows them to relax and forget their worries for a while. Second, a love for nature can encourage residents to protect the environment. People who value green spaces are more likely to save water, reduce waste and take part in activities such as planting trees. These small actions can make cities better places to live.

Therefore, both individuals and local governments should take action. Residents can spend more time outdoors and learn about plants and animals. Meanwhile, governments can build more parks and organize environmental activities. By working together, we can enjoy healthier lives and create greener cities.

**修改说明：**原文整体语法和结构清楚。本版主要将开头改为更自然的 `As urbanization continues`，并使用 `develop a greater appreciation for nature` 表达“更加欣赏、重视自然”。但真题明确要求作文以指定句开头，因此该润色版只用于比较表达，不能直接作为本题考场版本；正式作答必须保留原题指定开头。原来的 `develop a stronger love for nature` 可以理解；初学时先记 `develop a love for...` 即可。`vitally important` 语法正确，只是语气更强。不把润色选择当作唯一正确答案。

#### 写作结构与可迁移思路

| 段落 | 本篇功能 | 可以模仿的做法 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 第一段 | 城市发展背景 → 接触自然的机会减少 → 提出观点 | 先写一个具体变化，再说明为什么需要行动，不只写空泛的“非常重要” |
| 第二段 | 减轻压力 + 保护环境，两点理由 | 每个理由配一个例子或具体行为，如散步、节水、植树 |

本篇的 `First / Second / For example / Therefore / Meanwhile` 各有作用。模仿时保留逻辑，不必把所有连接词都机械塞入每一篇作文。

#### 本篇优先背诵清单（12 项）

1. it is important for somebody to do something（对某人来说，做某事很重要）
2. develop a love for...（培养对……的热爱）
3. spend time in nature（在自然中度过时间／亲近自然）
4. have fewer chances to do something（有更少的机会做某事）
5. reduce stress（减轻压力）
6. allow somebody to do something（允许／使某人有条件做某事）
7. encourage somebody to do something（鼓励／促使某人做某事）
8. protect the environment（保护环境）
9. be more likely to do something（更有可能做某事）
10. take part in...（参加……）
11. take action to do something（采取行动做某事）
12. by working together（通过共同努力）

先背这12项；`urbanization`、`appreciation` 等拓展词可稍后复习。旧材料中的 `help somebody do / to do something` 与 `make it + adjective + to do something` 在本篇继续复现，已在总表合并来源。

<a id="writing-notes-002"></a>
#### 关键语法与易错点

1. **`it is important for somebody to do something`。** `it` 是形式主语，后面的不定式说明具体事项：`It is important for residents to protect nature.` 练习本句型时不要漏掉 `for` 或 `to`，不能写 `It is important for residents protect nature.` 原文 `vitally important` 中的 `vitally` 是副词，修饰形容词 `important`；不能改成 `vital important`。
2. **`develop` 的新用法。** 在001中可表示发展、制定；本篇 `develop a love for nature` 指逐渐形成或培养情感，不是“开发自然”。`development` 是名词，不能代替动词：`We can develop a love for reading.`
3. **`spend` 后面的结构。** `spend time indoors / outdoors` 用地点副词；`spend time in nature` 用介词短语；花时间做事用 `spend time doing something`，不用 `spend time to do something`。`outdoors` 是副词，`outdoor` 是形容词：`spend time outdoors` / `outdoor activities`。
4. **`fewer` 与 `less`。** `chances` 是复数可数名词，因此用 `fewer chances`；`time` 在此不可数，因此用 `less time`。不写 `less chances`。
5. **`make it necessary to do something`。** 与001的 `make it easier for somebody to do something` 共用 `make + it + 形容词 + (for + 人) + to do` 结构；`for + 人` 可按需要省略。`This makes it necessary to bring nature back into our daily lives.` 中 `it` 是形式宾语；`This` 作主语，谓语用 `makes`。
6. **`help / allow / encourage / enable` 的区别。** `help somebody do / to do` 两种都可用；`allow / encourage / enable somebody to do` 在这些句型中都保留 `to`。它们分别侧重帮助、许可或提供条件、鼓励、使能够，不能因为中文接近就任意互换。
7. **动名词短语作主语。** `Spending time in nature helps people relax.` 和 `Taking a walk allows them to relax.` 中，整个动作短语通常看作单数，分别用 `helps`、`allows`。原文 `spending time ... can help` 中，情态动词 `can` 后用动词原形。
8. **`be more likely to do` 表示可能性。** `People who value green spaces are more likely to protect them.` 中 `people` 是复数，关系从句用 `value`，主句用 `are`；该表达不等于“必然会”。
9. **并列与举例。** 原文 `save water, reduce waste and take part in...` 三个动词形式并列；`such as planting trees` 中用动名词。对比：`outdoor activities such as walking` / `For example, residents can walk in a park.`
10. **`by` 后接动名词。** `By working together, we can create greener cities.` 不用 `by work` 或 `by to work`；共同努力的人是后面的 `we`。换主语时也要保证逻辑一致。
11. **让某地变得宜居。** `make cities better places to live` 是 `make + 宾语 + 名词补足语`：复数 `cities` 对应 `places`；单数可写 `make our city a better place to live`。
12. **词形与连接词。** `environment` 是名词，`environmental` 是形容词；用 `protect the environment`、`environmental activities`。`Therefore` 表示因果，`Meanwhile` 表示同时或并行，不合并成同一种逻辑连接词。

## 万能写作句型

优先学习自然、可迁移的搭配，结合[002写作结构与可迁移思路](#writing-outline-002)使用，不机械堆叠句型。

| 表达或句型 | 中文含义 | 结构说明 | 例句 | 可迁移主题 | 来源材料编号 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| as urbanization continues | 随着城市化持续推进 | 参考润色版开头；`as + 主语 + 谓语` 在此表示“随着……”。 | As urbanization continues, cities need more parks. | 城市发展、交通、公共服务 | 002 |
| it is (vitally) important for somebody to do something | 对某人来说，做某事（极其）重要 | `it` 为形式主语；`for + 人` 后接 `to + 动词原形`；`vitally` 修饰 `important`，可省略。 | It is important for students to review regularly. | 教育、健康、环保、工作 | 002 |
| develop a love / appreciation for... | 培养对……的热爱／欣赏 | `develop + 名词 + for + 对象`；`love` 与 `appreciation` 含义有差别，按需要选择；后者来自参考润色版。 | Children can develop a love for reading. | 教育、文化、自然、兴趣培养 | 002 |
| spend time in... / outdoors / doing something | 在……度过时间／在户外活动／花时间做某事 | 地点用 `in + 地点` 或副词 `outdoors`；做某事用 `spend + 时间 + doing`，不用 `spend time to do`。 | Students can spend more time reading outdoors. | 教育、健康、休闲、时间管理 | 002 |
| have fewer chances to do something | 有更少的机会做某事 | `chances` 为可数名词复数，用 `fewer`；比较不可数名词用 `less`，如 `less time`。 | Busy parents have fewer chances to exercise. | 健康、工作、教育、城市生活 | 002 |
| bring...back into our daily lives | 把……重新带回日常生活 | `bring + 对象 + back into + 范围`；可替换中间名词。 | We can bring traditional culture back into our daily lives. | 文化、自然、社区、生活方式 | 002 |
| reduce stress | 减轻压力 | `reduce + 名词`；`stress` 在此通常作不可数名词，不写 `reduce stressful`。 | Regular exercise can reduce stress. | 健康、教育、工作 | 002 |
| allow somebody to do something | 允许某人做某事；使某人有条件做某事 | `allow + 人 + to + 动词原形`；本篇偏“使有条件”，具体语境也可表示许可。 | Flexible working hours allow parents to care for their children. | 工作、教育、科技、健康 | 002 |
| encourage somebody to do something | 鼓励／促使某人做某事 | `encourage + 人 + to + 动词原形`；强调鼓励、促使，不能漏掉 `to`。 | Reading activities encourage students to read more. | 教育、环保、健康、社区 | 002 |
| protect the environment | 保护环境 | 固定搭配通常用 `the environment`；名词表达为 `environmental protection`。 | We should protect the environment in our daily lives. | 环保、城市发展、公共责任 | 002 |
| be more likely to do something | 更有可能做某事 | `be + more likely + to + 动词原形`；表达可能性，不表示必然发生。 | Students who review regularly are more likely to remember new words. | 教育、健康、行为习惯 | 002 |
| take part in... | 参加…… | `in` 后接活动名词或动名词；不写 `take part in to do`。 | Residents can take part in planting trees. | 环保、校园活动、社区、志愿服务 | 002 |
| such as + noun / doing | 例如……（列举具体例子） | `such as` 后接名词或动名词；`for example` 则可引出一个完整例句，两者不直接混用。 | We can enjoy outdoor activities such as walking and cycling. | 环保、教育、健康、兴趣爱好 | 002 |
| make...a better place / better places to live | 使……成为更宜居的地方 | `make + 宾语 + 名词短语`；单数地区配 `a better place`，复数城市配 `better places`。 | More green spaces can make our community a better place to live. | 城市发展、社区、环保、公共服务 | 002 |
| take action (to do something) | 采取行动（做某事） | 通常用单数 `action` 表示行动；可接 `to + 动词原形` 说明目的。 | We should take action to reduce waste. | 环保、健康、教育、社会问题 | 002 |
| by doing something / by working together | 通过做某事／通过共同努力 | `by` 是介词，后接动名词，不用 `by do` 或 `by to do`；句首使用时注意动作主体一致。 | By working together, we can improve our community. | 社区、环保、团队协作、教育 | 002 |
| meanwhile | 与此同时 | 用于连接同时发生或并行推进的事情；不等同于表示因果的 `therefore`。 | Residents can save water. Meanwhile, governments can improve public services. | 城市、教育、环保、公共服务 | 002 |
| therefore | 因此 | 表示前后因果；句首常加逗号。 | Regular practice helps us learn. Therefore, we should practise every day. | 教育、健康、工作、论证写作 | 002 |
